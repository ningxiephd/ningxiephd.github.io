Most of my community work takes a similar form: someone is navigating an unfamiliar system, and I listen first, then help them find a way through it. I have done this as a peer-support volunteer, a university program manager, and an event organizer. My community engagement work is guided by three principles: listening, building trust, and connecting people to opportunity.

<h2 style="border-left: 6px solid #18453B; background-color: #edf4f0; color: #18453B; padding: 12px 16px; margin-top: 2em; margin-bottom: 1.2em;">Community Engagement in Practice</h2>

<h3 style="border-left: 3px solid #91b5a3; padding-left: 13px; margin-top: 1.6em;">Volunteering to Support Marginalized Communities</h3>

Since 2018, I have volunteered with Ark of Compassion, a nonprofit organization serving marginalized Asian communities. I have participated in peer support meetings for Asian immigrants experiencing difficult life circumstances. I have received introductory training in hospice volunteer work.

<h3 style="border-left: 3px solid #91b5a3; padding-left: 13px; margin-top: 1.6em;">Student and Stakeholder Engagement</h3>

I worked extensively with students from diverse cultural and socioeconomic backgrounds, particularly international students from Africa and Asia. I provided guidance to help students navigate their journey from admission to career opportunities. I served as a university representative at educational and community events, engaging prospective students, families, businesses, and community members.

<h3 style="border-left: 3px solid #91b5a3; padding-left: 13px; margin-top: 1.6em;">Bringing People Together</h3>

I organized a fundraising effort for a former teacher diagnosed with leukemia. I brought together more than 40 former classmates and held a recognition gathering. This event attracted national and local media attention that generated additional donations.

I hosted a university panel on graduate education and career pathways that attracted more than 300 attendees. The event brought together speakers, students, faculty, staff, and local business sponsors.

<h2 style="border-left: 6px solid #18453B; background-color: #edf4f0; color: #18453B; padding: 12px 16px; margin-top: 2em; margin-bottom: 1.2em;">My Approach to Community Partnership</h2>

I am a first-generation immigrant, and I have worked as an educator, a program professional, and a volunteer. These experiences shape how I work with people from different backgrounds. Professional expertise and lived experience are different kinds of knowledge, and a project needs both.

In community-engaged research, this means communities help set research priorities, contribute what they know, take part in decisions, and benefit from the findings. I hope to continue building partnerships that connect research with community priorities and translate evidence into meaningful action.
