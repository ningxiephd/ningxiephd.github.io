I am a communication scholar who also works on the program side of the university: managing programs, building international partnerships, and supporting research funding and proposal development. My work bridges scholarly inquiry and strategic administration by connecting researchers, funding opportunities, and program management to build collaborative initiatives.

### Experience Highlights

- **Program Management:** Nearly five years of experience in university program development and operations at the University of Louisville, collaborating with academic leadership, administrative units, and international partners to support graduate programs and institutional partnerships.
- **Research Development:** Hands-on experience in external funding analysis, proposal preparation (PI/Co-PI), and interdisciplinary team collaboration.
- **Research:** Collaboration with a multidisciplinary, grant-funded health research team since 2019.
- **Teaching & Service:** Taught at the University of Maryland, College Park. Taught at Michigan State University and served on MSU’s Undergraduate Research Advisory Council.

### Research and Professional Interests

My scholarly work examines emotions, trust, and crisis communication. I am particularly interested in cancer prevention communication and the role of trust in shaping public understanding of health risks.

Across all research and administrative endeavors, I aim to translate evidence-based knowledge into effective programs, equitable partnerships, and actionable impact.
