Current Research Focus

My research program centers on trust in health and risk communication: how communication features, institutions, social environments, and emerging technologies shape trust, risk perception, and health decisions. Current and emerging contexts include cancer prevention communication, alcohol and substance risk communication, and community crisis resilience.

Connecting Research with Action

I am most interested in collaborative work that brings researchers, program teams, institutions, funders, and communities together around consequential health and equity challenges. I value careful execution, clear communication, transparent decision making, strong relationships, and systems that turn experience into reusable knowledge.
