Grant & Research Development

From Opportunity to Organized Execution

I approach research development as a connected system: understand the opportunity, organize the team and work, maintain visibility during execution, and capture learning for what comes next.

My experience combines university program operations, external proposal development, and multidisciplinary health research. I have not yet administered a national grantmaking portfolio. What I bring is strong adjacent experience in complex program coordination, proposal development, research collaboration, documentation, stakeholder follow through, and the design of practical systems that make work visible and manageable.

How I Organize Complex Work

1 · ClarifyDefine purpose, requirements, roles, decision points, constraints, and what success needs to look like.
2 · CoordinateTranslate priorities into responsibilities, timelines, meetings, documents, review points, and follow up.
3 · Make VisibleUse practical trackers and dashboards to surface milestones, reporting status, risks, decisions, and next actions.
4 · Learn & ReuseCapture lessons, templates, decision records, training materials, and process improvements for the next cycle.

Program Management Toolkit

Operating Rhythm

Integrated calendars, agendas, responsibility tracking, milestone reviews, issue logs, decision records, and clear handoffs across teams and workstreams.

Portfolio Visibility

Concise dashboards that organize milestones, deliverables, reporting, risks, support needs, decisions, and next actions for leadership review.

Knowledge Infrastructure

Shared repositories, common templates, SOPs, training archives, reusable guidance, and after action learning that reduce duplication and preserve institutional knowledge.

Selected Experience

University Program Development and Operations

At the University of Louisville College of Business, I supported nearly five years of program growth and operations across graduate, professional, certificate, and executive offerings. I coordinated across Admissions, Student Services, International Affairs, Career Services, marketing, academic leadership, external universities, education organizations, business partners, professional groups, and community facing events. As part of the program team, I supported more than 300 annual enrollments during a period of approximately 200% enrollment growth.

External Proposal Development

I served as Co PI on a $100,000 Sony Research Award proposal and as PI on an Arthur W. Page Center foundation grant proposal. Across these efforts, I developed or supported project rationale, scope of work, methods, timelines, budgets and budget justification, data collection plans, dissemination plans, and submission materials. These are proposal development experiences and are not presented as funded awards.

Funded Multidisciplinary Health Research

Since 2019, I have contributed to a multidisciplinary, multinational research collaboration conducted within a VA Biomedical Laboratory Research and Development Merit funded program. My work includes literature synthesis, quantitative analysis, manuscript and project material development, substantive editing, and coordination across contributors and institutions.

Funding Intelligence & Research Development

I maintain an active funding intelligence practice across foundations, federal agencies, and other research sponsors. In health communication and prevention, I follow NIH, NCI, NIAAA, and relevant philanthropic priorities and translate emerging opportunities into concrete research questions, team needs, proposal components, and development plans.

Operating principle: complex programs work better when strategy, execution, documentation, and learning are connected. The goal is not more process. It is enough structure to create clarity, accountability, continuity, and useful learning without unnecessary burden.
